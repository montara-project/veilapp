A macOS menu bar app that keeps your menu bar clean and tidy. Veil App lives in the menu bar: it lists every running menu bar app with its live memory usage, and hides the icons you don't want to see behind a one-click wall — a lightweight alternative to Hidden Bar and Ice.

You're up to date. Veil App checks for updates when it starts.
